Meet Our Farmers Series · Part II · In Person

Livestock Farming in the Hudson Valley

Saturday, May 16, 2026  ·  2:00 – 5:00 PM
Staatsburg, NY

Livestock farming in the Hudson Valley, NY is heavily focused on sustainable, pasture-raised, and regenerative agriculture, producing high-quality grass-fed beef, dairy, pork, lamb, chickens as well as turkey, geese, quail and rabbits. Farms emphasize animal welfare and ecological restoration, sometimes offering related farm products, education and farm visits. This event will take place in the Barn at Hudson Hollow Farm and will feature several local farming operations, showcasing Wild Lea Farm's on site operations with sheep, rabbits, geese and quail. We will hear directly from farmers about the pasture raised approach, combining different livestock in one system, processing challenges and landowner partnerships. Following conversations and Q&A, we will visit Wild Lea Farm's livestock, walk through fields to observe grazing practices first hand, connect with other farmland owners and enjoy refreshments in the Barn.

This year, Hudson Valley Farmland Stewards' "Meet our Farmers" series of events is focused on connecting farmland owners with farmers to learn more about farming in the Hudson Valley and to develop a supportive dialogue that facilitates successful farming operations on leased land.
